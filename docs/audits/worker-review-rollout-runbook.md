After the final locked preflight and effective-unit check, **release the retained lock before starting the service** so its own scheduler can acquire it. Start only the reviewed service once with a blocking start and wait within its accepted operating bounds; never enable or start the timer. Establish service quiescence first: inactive state, no pending job, no main/control PID and no live process in the service cgroup. Then reacquire the same lock before inspecting the resulting retained state. The supervisor writes status before acquiring the scheduler lock, so reacquiring that lock alone cannot establish service quiescence. An `already_running` result, a skipped start condition, timeout or stale status is a failed acceptance, not an idle cycle.

The private success receipt must bind all of these observations to the same exact service invocation:

- Evidence that the installed entrypoint actually ran and exited successfully. A successful blocking start of the exact same service, the exact consumed ticket and fresh exact supervisor status together are sufficient captured invocation evidence. A surviving `InvocationID` or `ExecMain` result may corroborate this but is optional after legitimate systemd garbage collection; no additional pinning or journal mechanism is required. A default cached `Result=success` alone is insufficient.
- The ticket was consumed, and the observed supervisor outcome is `status=complete`, `cycles=1`, `processed=0`, `review_state=idle`. These are required observed values, established by the invocation and fresh status evidence; a journal transcript of the printed result is not required.
- The freshly written current status is `state=idle`, `cycle_status=complete`, `cycles=1`, `processed=0`; review counts show exactly one verified record and zero other states. Bind freshness to this attempt and its baseline, not just a matching old JSON value.
- The retained database, reviewer key, admissions/accounting and journals compare unchanged, with no provider request or candidate execution. The persistent unit, installed disabled config and location descriptor still match their accepted baseline.

Current status is the expected operational output of the idle invocation. Preserve it and the private receipt; do not overwrite previous one-off acceptance evidence. If any retained state unexpectedly changes, preserve the actual resulting state, record the discrepancy and stop. **Never restore a database/key snapshot, rewind spent admissions or erase a journal to make the comparison pass.**

### Cleanup, interruption and remaining gates

While holding the retained lock after the invocation has ended, remove only the exact temporary drop-in, config and ticket objects created by this controller, after checking their identities. Do not recursively delete shared runtime directories or other owners' drop-ins. Keep the once marker and protected evidence. Reload systemd and confirm the accepted persistent files and effective settings: original guard `NO`, installed `execution_authorized=false`, no temporary override, inactive service with no pending job, and disabled/inactive timer. Record these observations in the final receipt. Do not restart the service to prove that it is disabled.

If execution, lock reacquisition or cleanup cannot be confirmed, report the observed failure and leave the relevant evidence for reconciliation. An interrupted run has no successful-restoration claim until the owned runtime objects, service state and retained journals have been inspected and the final disabled-state receipt is complete. Do not automatically rerun the controller, rearm the ticket or restore a database. Returning to disabled service settings must preserve actual retained state even after an unsuccessful invocation.

For error cleanup, a failed service is quiescent when it has no pending job, no main/control PID and no live process in its cgroup. Do not treat `systemctl stop` as evidence that the failed state was cleared. After proving that quiescence and reacquiring the retained lock, the controller may remove its exact temporary config/drop-in and restore the original guard `NO`, while preserving the failed service state and its evidence. Report **BLOCKED**, with cleanup observations recorded separately from idle acceptance. Do not retain temporary execution authority merely because the processless unit is failed, and do not issue an automatic `reset-failed` or another start to obtain a clean status.

Passing this contract accepts only the installed credential-free idle path. It does not demonstrate productive generation, revision, external-provider access or end-to-end work within the service time budgets. Productive work needs a separate concrete bounded task, grant and credential scope; recurring scheduling is a separate decision. **P1-SCHEDULED-SERVICE-BOUND-01, the broader P1 package and P0-OPS-RECON-01 / Issue #90 remain OPEN.**
